# Research workflows

## Apparent market move

Supply permitted prices or spreads with actual observation times, explicit
calendar gaps and an instrument-appropriate freshness limit. Prices become log
returns; spreads keep their units. Inspect departure, persistence and volatility
alongside visibility gaps. A review flag does not establish predictability,
materiality or profitability. Corporate actions and market closures matter.

## Headline overload

Supply event IDs, titles, entities, sources and publication/availability clocks.
Set focus entities and an attention budget. All original events remain available;
copied wording is grouped, changed quantities and denials stay separate, and
reports sharing an event key can be marked for review. Copies do not become
independent confirmation. Inspect the primary document for a material decision.

## LiquiLens institution review

Keep each bank's metrics separate by unit, source and reporting period. Preserve
revision availability. Apply quarterly filing expectations to quarterly evidence,
not an intraday-price rule. NoiseFloor describes changes and coverage gaps;
LiquiLens retains its institution-evidence and validation authority.

## Seiche funding and Undertow liquidity

Preserve source-specific funding clocks and venue-specific liquidity observations.
Common movements are not independent confirmation. A missing venue can alter
measurement coverage; trading volume itself remains an economic series.

## Private AI agent

Use Python or MCP with explicit inputs/policy. Route `insufficient_visibility` to
coverage repair, `review` to evidence review, and `watch` to the caller's monitoring
plan. Save complete JSON and request/policy digests. A quiet diagnostic never
authorizes an order; routing remains a separate permissioned system.

## Demonstrate value

Freeze a universe, window, policy and reviewer criteria. Retain original source
publication/revision times. Compare with a simple baseline and measure missed
material events, review volume, time to review and data gaps across quiet and
stressful periods. Publish all misses and outages. Synthetic examples verify
behavior; they do not replace a forward evaluation with real reviewers.
